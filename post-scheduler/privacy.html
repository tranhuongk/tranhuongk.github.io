Privacy Policy

Personal Post Scheduler · Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. What this app is

Personal Post Scheduler is a single-owner tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts. It has no public users and no sign-up.

2. Data accessed through TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope;
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload) and, where granted, to publish videos (video.publish).

The tool does not read private messages, followers, other people's content, or any data outside these scopes.

3. How data is stored

Access and refresh tokens, and the queue of posts, are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server operated by this service, and no analytics or advertising trackers are used.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is sent only to TikTok, and only to carry out an upload the owner approved.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the owner's computer until deleted. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services, and can delete the local data directly.

6. Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13 and is not offered to the public.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
