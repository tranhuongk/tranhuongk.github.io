Personal Post Scheduler

A personal tool for scheduling and uploading short videos.

What it does

Personal Post Scheduler lets its owner queue short videos and upload them to the owner's own TikTok account, either as a draft to finish inside the TikTok app or as a scheduled post. The owner reviews and approves every post before it is sent.

Who it is for

This is a single-owner tool. It is not offered to the public and does not publish content on behalf of other people.
